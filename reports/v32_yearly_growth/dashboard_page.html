Money in vs portfolio value, week by week

Each chart is a fresh start that year: ₹1 lakh into every weekly V3.2 list for the first 26 lists, so money in stops at ₹26 lakh. Each lot is sold 6 months after purchase and the money goes into that week's new list. Value includes trading costs and dividends. 2019 starts in April, when the data starts; 2026 runs to the latest list. Same scale on every chart.

Return on money put in, month by month

Portfolio value ÷ money put in − 1, at each month's last close. Same scale on every chart; the dashed line is break-even.

Backtest of the V3.2 rule: lists rebuilt with today's data and model, bought at the list-day close (buying at Monday's open was about 2–2.5 points a year lower), liquidity-scaled trading costs of 0.5–2% per round trip, dividends counted, before tax. The rule was chosen after seeing these years, so the future should come in lower.
