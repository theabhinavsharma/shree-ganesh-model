## 4. Ignition signatures

- **[28]** GRIND-THEN-DETONATE: weeks of +3-8% days on 1-3x volume with healthy delivery (35-60%) off the trough, then a scheduled-event detonation (results/order day, 5-15x volume, delivery collapsing to 10-25%). The modal doubler pattern.
- **[15]** NO-NEWS VOLUME BURST: a +8-19% day on 3-15x volume with delivery collapsing to ~10-25% and NO matching filing (often drawing an unanswered exchange query); hard news arrives 3-8 weeks later. Informed-money / front-run footprint.
- **[14]** SINGLE EVENT-DAY REPRICING: dead or sideways base (sometimes months), then one post-results gap (+8-20%, 5-17x volume) does most of the double; run continues as post-thrust momentum.
- **[10]** GAP-ON-ANNOUNCEMENT LADDER: discrete disclosed-news gaps (order wins, M&A, licences) each followed by digestion — filings hit the exchange on or 1-2 sessions before each leg; chaseable at next open.
- **[9]** CIRCUIT LADDER / LOW-FLOAT MELT-UP: stacked ~5% band-limit or 20% UC closes, often on THIN or drying volume (STLTECH melted to 2x on 0.1-0.7x volume) with near-zero delivery — squeeze mechanics, weakest fundamental anchoring, sharpest round-trips (VIDYAWIRES, IRMENERGY, WEL).

Tape stats: 78% printed a ≥+5% day on ≥1.5× vol within 15 sessions of trough; median first thrust session 5, price +13.9% off low, **median +76% still left to 2x after the thrust close**.
