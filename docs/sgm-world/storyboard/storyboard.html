SGM Data World

A rough preview of the new home page for shreeganeshmodel.vercel.app: a real Earth where little bots carry every input of the model to a laptop in Bengaluru. These are real screenshots of the build on the laptop, step by step, following your drawings.

Not live. The site shows the old page until you say go.

The journey

Plays when a visitor taps Start the journey. Each step waits for Next; anyone can skip to exploring.

1

Meet the crew

Seven bots line up in lanes, like your drawing: each lane shows the bot's name, the data it brings and its home country, and each bot wears that country's flag as its T-shirt.

Moves: on Go! they all start running to the right and off the screen, then the Earth takes over.

2

Zoom out: they set off from home

Several camera angles. Fed runs out of the St. Louis Fed with oil and world prices toward the US East Coast; Euro leaves the European Central Bank in Frankfurt for the coast at Marseille.

Moves: bots run along glowing amber paths and leave a trail; the sky is the real sky at the moment you visit (daytime in St. Louis, night in Frankfurt here).

3

They board the ships

Ship 1, an oil tanker, carries oil and world prices across the Atlantic and the Red Sea to Mumbai; the submarine marks the undersea cable. Ship 2, a container ship, brings Claude's Friday note and world headlines across the Pacific to Chennai.

Moves: ships sail the routes and leave a white wake. In real life the data travels through undersea fibre cables; the ships are the cartoon of that, and routes are simplified.

4

India

Ship 1 docks in Mumbai and Ship 2 in Chennai. NSE in Mumbai adds today's stock prices; PIB in New Delhi, next to Parliament, adds government news. Every path heads to Bengaluru.

Moves: couriers fly the paths with their crates; cyan paths carry your Telegram messages, green paths carry money.

5

Bengaluru

You, as the giant beside the laptop with the stocks chart, while ML bots with hammers turn the data into the Friday list. Money moves between your SBI account (head office, Mumbai) and your stocks.

Moves: you wave, the ML bots hammer, ₹ notes fly both ways. Shown at Vidhana Soudha, a public landmark, never a home address.
