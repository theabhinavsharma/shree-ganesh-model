# SGM Data World: game design rules

These are the rules a game designer at Nintendo or Epic would hold this universe to. Every scene and every new piece gets checked against them.

## 1. Nothing invisible
Every action has a cause you can see and an effect you can see.
- **Handing over data:** the crate is **thrown** in an arc. The receiver crouches, **catches** it and bounces.
- **The ML bots hammering:** the crate sits on an **anvil**. Each hit squashes it, throws sparks and gives the camera a small shake. After a few hits it turns into the **Friday list**, a paper scroll.
- **The laptop checking data:** the crate drops into the laptop. Ticks appear on screen one by one, up to "21 checks".
- **Telegram:** the phone **buzzes**, wiggles and pops a notification card.
- **Holding stocks for 6 months:** a calendar flips its pages.
- **Money moving:** ₹ notes flutter and spin. They don't slide.

## 2. Motion has weight
Animation principles: anticipation, squash and stretch, follow-through, easing, arcs.
- **Before a run:** a short crouch.
- **While running:** dust puffs at the feet.
- **On landing:** a squash.
- **Thrown things:** they follow a gravity arc and spin.
- **Ships:** they bob and roll, leave a white wake, and push a bow splash. The submarine leaves bubbles.
- **Mixed speeds:** fast things (couriers) and slow things (ships) move at different speeds, so the eye reads scale.

## 3. Every stop is a place, not a signpost
Each stop is a small diorama of the real place, in toy style:
- Exchange Plaza in BKC
- The St. Louis Fed
- The twin glass ECB towers in Frankfurt
- PIB with its dome and flag
- A port with a gantry crane
- A cable-landing hut whose cable runs visibly into the sea
- Your desk with the laptop
- The phone stand
- SBI's tower and the broker's kiosk

Local dressing places them: palms for Mumbai and Chennai, oaks for St. Louis and Frankfurt.

## 4. One look, one scale
- **Sizes:** a bot is 1 unit tall, buildings are 3 to 6, ships about 6 on stage.
- **Colours always mean the same thing:**
  - amber for data
  - cyan for messages
  - green for money
  - orange for the bots
- **Light:** soft shadows ground everything; nothing floats without a shadow.
- **Sky:** follows the real time of day.

## 5. Camera tells the story
- One focus at a time.
- During a run, the camera leads slightly ahead.
- During work (hammering, checking), it moves closer.
- It shakes a little on impact.
- Labels never cover the action.

## 6. Feedback and reward
- Checkpoints light up as the crate passes them.
- The progress dots fill in.
- The end of a journey lands on a payoff: the list arrives, you cheer, the phone buzzes.

## 7. The universe runs on a clock
- The world has its own rhythm:
  - the 6:45 PM weekday run, when every data bot leaves at once
  - the Friday 7:30 PM list
  - Monday's order
  - the 6-month sale
- **Next step:** live status. When a feed fails, its bot shows it, for example sitting down with a red light.

## 8. UI stays out of the way
- **The crew:** a slim strip of tiny characters.
- **Full roster:** a liquid-glass sheet that opens on demand.
- **Cards:** the plain-words card is short. Details are folded away.
